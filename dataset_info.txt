DATASET INFORMATION
===================

Name:
  HR Analytics: Job Change of Data Scientists

Kaggle URL:
  https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/arashnic/hr-analytics-job-change-of-data-scientists

License:
  CC0: Public Domain
  (Verify on the Kaggle dataset page before submission.)

File used:
  aug_train.csv  (saved in this repo as raw_kaggle_data.csv)
  19,158 records, 14 columns

Original purpose:
  Designed to understand the factors that lead a person to leave their current
  job, for HR research. A company that trains data scientists wants to know
  which enrollees will stay and which will look for a new job, so it can
  reduce hiring/training costs and plan its workforce and courses.

Target variable:
  target  ->  0 = Not looking for a job change
              1 = Looking for a job change

Columns:
  enrollee_id            Unique candidate ID (identifier, not a predictor)
  city                   City code
  city_development_index Development index of the city (scaled)
  gender                 Gender of candidate
  relevent_experience    Relevant experience (yes/no)
  enrolled_university    University course enrollment type, if any
  education_level        Education level
  major_discipline       Education major discipline
  experience             Total experience in years
  company_size           Number of employees in current employer
  company_type           Type of current employer
  last_new_job           Years between previous job and current job
  training_hours         Training hours completed
  target                 Job-change label (see above)

Known data characteristics:
  - Missing values in several categorical columns
  - Imbalanced class distribution (class 1 is the minority)
  - Mixed categorical and numeric features
